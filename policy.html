1. Introduction & Mission

SpringWave ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a digital platform built by and for students across universities. Our mission is to democratize discovery of extracurricular activities, workshops, competitions, skill roadmaps, and community interactions.

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. This Policy explains how we collect, handle, protect, and delete your personal information, including information accessed when you choose to use Single Sign-On (SSO) such as Sign in with Google.

Primary Contact Entity:

SpringWave Digital Education Platform (RAH Team) • Da Nang, Vietnam

Official Website: https://springwave.io.vn

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide and enhance our educational services.

When you sign in using "Sign in with Google", we request access to basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). This grants us access to:

5. Data Storage, HMAC Architecture & Security

We employ enterprise-grade security protocols. All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3.

Mutating requests are signed with client-side Web Crypto HMAC-SHA256 signatures (X-Timestamp, X-Nonce, and X-Signature) ensuring zero replay attacks and tampering protection.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Guide

You retain absolute ownership and control over your personal data at all times.

settings Option 1: Self-Service Deletion

Go to Profile

Visit your SpringWave Profile page, click "Account Settings", and select "Delete Account". All your personal data and linked tokens will be permanently purged.

mail Option 2: Direct Privacy Email Request

Compose Email

Send an email to springwave150626@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" from your registered email address. We will verify and process your deletion within 30 days.

cancel Option 3: Revoke Access in Google Settings

Google Dashboard open_in_new

You can immediately revoke SpringWave's permissions at any time via your Google Account Security Dashboard (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

9. Student & Youth Privacy Protections

SpringWave is designed for higher-education students. We do not intentionally collect information from individuals under 16 years of age. Student ID verification photos are encrypted, stored in restricted buckets, and reviewed only by authorized administrators.
