THE ORIGIN

Built from real student frustration.

“We missed workshops and competitions not because we lacked passion, but because information was scattered across dozens of random fanpages and chat groups.”

As first-year university students in Da Nang, we experienced firsthand how difficult it was to navigate extracurricular life. Crucial announcements, workshop sign-ups, and student club recruitments were constantly buried under social media algorithms.

We founded RAH TEAM with a clear purpose: create a single, clean, student-centric home where every event, discussion, and roadmap is structured, searchable, and verified.

01. Centralized
Every workshop & hackathon structured in one unified database.
02. Verified
Authenticated student profiles & trustworthy organizers.
